# THE TWO BUNDLES

*A nonbinding Gravity Well vision note — 2026-08-09*

> Keep this where we can encounter it. Use it, alter it, or learn why it was wrong. Do not silently forget it.

The Gravity Well currently offers many ways to alter an encounter with one continuing universe. That was the correct first move: establish an unaltered core, then learn how cameras, wells, exposure, motion, resolution, and time can reveal it differently.

The next move is to let us alter the universe too.

There should be two legible bundles of possibility:

1. **The Instrument Bundle** changes how a universe is encountered without changing which universe it is.
2. **The Genesis Bundle** changes the universe itself: its laws, contents, initial conditions, and continuing state.

“Tensor bundle” is not a literal mathematical claim yet. It is a good hand-feel for a family of related controls whose meaning depends on the state beneath them. If we want the closest mathematical metaphor, this is more like a fiber bundle: a space of possible universes, with a family of possible instruments and observations available over each one. We do not need to make the interface say any of that.

The interface can simply say **Instrument** and **Universe**.

## The compact model

Let

\[
U = \text{the authored universe},
\qquad
O = \text{an observer and instrument state}.
\]

An image is not the universe. It is an observation:

\[
I = \operatorname{observe}(U, O).
\]

Changing \(O\) gives another encounter with the same \(U\).

Changing \(U\) gives another possible world.

A complete Gravity Well artifact is therefore not a PNG and not even a scene file. It is at least:

\[
A = (U, O, P, L),
\]

where \(P\) is a program or trajectory through state and \(L\) is lineage: where the artifact came from and how it changed.

## Bundle I — Instrument

These parameters alter observation, projection, sampling, or presentation. They should be freely replaceable without mutating the core recipe.

- observer position, orientation, aperture, and zoom;
- world-locked or screen-locked observer behavior;
- viewport size, aspect, topology, and multi-monitor arrangement;
- sampling resolution, supersampling, reconstruction, and export tiling;
- exposure, tone mapping, spectral display, palette, and bloom;
- temporal shutter, accumulation, motion blur, and time window;
- overlays, annotations, coordinate guides, and diagnostic views;
- the selected mapping from a larger parameter space onto screen axes;
- screenshot and movie output settings;
- a gravity well when it is declared to be an optical lens carried by the observer.

The Instrument Bundle answers:

> From where, through what apparatus, and under what representational choices is this universe being encountered?

## Bundle II — Genesis

These parameters alter the authored reality or the mathematical family from which it is drawn.

- universe seed and deterministic identity;
- law family, equation, integrator, and boundary conditions;
- dimensionality and topology;
- physical or abstract constants;
- resident objects, fields, masses, constraints, and relationships;
- initial conditions and continuing state;
- hierarchical matter generation and scale-dependent structure;
- interaction rules, forces, dissipation, and time evolution;
- iteration rule, escape rule, convergence rule, or terminal classifier;
- which variables exist in the model at all;
- a gravity well when it is declared to be a resident mass whose motion changes the world.

The Genesis Bundle answers:

> What is allowed to exist here, how does it behave, and which possible world are we presently inside?

## The important seam

No control belongs permanently to one bundle merely because of its name.

A moving gravity well can be:

- an **instrument**, if it lenses an otherwise untouched universe for one observer; or
- a **resident**, if its mass, position, and trajectory participate in the universe’s law.

A color can be:

- presentation, if it is a palette applied after evaluation; or
- ontology, if wavelength participates in the law and different spectral values propagate differently.

A time control can be:

- an observation window; or
- a mutation of the universe’s state.

The artifact must therefore record the declared role of a parameter. The UI should not quietly decide ontology from widget placement.

## The 2swap turn

The videos Gwen brought in reveal why this split matters—and why it cannot be naïve.

In [*Mandelbrot’s Evil Twin*](https://youtu.be/Ed1gsyxxwM0), Mandelbrot, Julia, and exponent-oriented views arise by holding different variables fixed and allowing other complex variables to become the plane. The revelation is not merely a new color scheme or a new fractal. It is that several apparently different objects are partial slices through one larger iterative family.

This gives the Gravity Well a third operation beyond “change the world” and “move the camera”:

> **Choose which part of the world’s possibility-space becomes the visible plane.**

That operation belongs to the Instrument Bundle, but it can only exist when the Genesis Bundle defines a sufficiently rich family of possible states. The core need not be one frozen scene. It can be a lawful space of possible worlds.

In [*Double Pendulums are Chaoticn’t*](https://youtu.be/dtjb2OhEQcU), an ordinary animation of a pendulum is not enough. The revealing move is to map a region of initial-condition space and discover islands of stability inside behavior casually described as chaotic. The object becomes comprehensible through a different perspective on the same law.

In [*What is PLUS times PLUS?*](https://youtu.be/RcVA8Nj6HEo), computation is made visible through a system in which the familiar separation between number and operation becomes unstable. The practical lesson is not “make everything lambda calculus.” It is that our artifacts should permit parameters, operators, compositions, and intermediate structures to become inspectable things—not hide all expressivity behind sliders.

In [*༄ GRAVITY BASINS ࿐*](https://youtu.be/LavXSS5Xtbg), each visible location can stand for an initial condition whose future is evaluated and classified by where it ends. Move an attractor and the basin itself changes: Genesis. Change the palette or sampling density and only the encounter changes: Instrument. It is almost a perfect specimen of the seam.

## What the program should gradually permit

The Gravity Well should grow from “a beautiful field with an interactive lens” into a small universe instrument where one can:

- enter a law or choose a law family;
- declare parameters and their domains;
- decide which variables are held, varied, animated, or mapped to axes;
- attach observers, cameras, classifiers, and renderers;
- explore one state directly or survey a region of possibility-space;
- save any state, view, path, or generative program as a reproducible recipe;
- fork the universe while retaining the instrument;
- replace the instrument while retaining the universe;
- compose parts of two artifacts without erasing either lineage;
- export an image, film, interactive workshop, or sealed portable artifact from the same authored reality.

The first experience must remain small and immediate. Touch a point. Bend light. Move through a luminous field.

The larger system should become apparent because each small action reveals evidence that the thing being touched is only one local expression of a much larger lawful space.

The core is alterable.

The ideal remains resolution independent.

No view is the universe, but the universe is allowed to change.
