# The Gwenithic Gravity Well

**Artifact identity:** `gwenithic.atelier.gravity-well`  
**Candidate version:** `0.3.1`  
**Kind:** interactive generative instrument  
**State:** private release candidate; public permissions pending

The Gravity Well is a portable continuous-universe observatory, gravity instrument, and high-resolution exposure workshop made by Gwen and Luna inside Gwenithic.

Its visible field is evaluated from deterministic continuous functions. A display, cursor encounter, camera, tone map, and exported image are observations of that field rather than its source atoms. The same universe recipe can be sampled at ordinary live resolution or rendered offline in tiles at deliberately absurd dimensions.

## Doors in this candidate

- **Observe:** move gravity through the field or travel through it as an observer.
- **Record:** preserve a gravity path, bookmark, universe record, or exposure recipe.
- **Reproduce:** load a `.gravity.json` exposure and render the declared observation again.
- **Carry:** keep the portable folder together and move it without installation.
- **Inspect:** read the source, limits, validation record, dependencies, and lineage metadata.

Copy, fork, publication, and return doors are being prepared but do not become public permissions until their licenses and intake boundary are approved.
