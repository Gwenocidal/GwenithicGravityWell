# Creator-Controlled Outputs

Gwenithic claims no ownership merely because an image, exposure recipe, universe journal, path, scene, or other output was produced with the Gravity Well.

The maker controls what permissions they are able to grant, subject to the rights carried by embedded material and the law governing authorship or copyrightability. Nothing in the application license automatically forces an output to use MIT, CC BY, CC0, or any other public license.

The default is therefore **no Gwenithic publication grant**.

If a body is returned to the Atelier, these remain separate decisions:

- whether Gwenithic may inspect it privately;
- whether Gwenithic may preserve a private working copy;
- whether Gwenithic may display it publicly;
- whether Gwenithic may redistribute the underlying files;
- whether anyone may adapt it;
- how the maker wishes to be attributed;
- what limits or contextual words must travel with it.

See `RETURN.md` for the first manual return protocol.
